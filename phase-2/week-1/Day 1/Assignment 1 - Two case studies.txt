Case Study 1: Oversee
---------------------
 1. What problem/use case did they have?

Oversee's support and operations teams spent a lot of time manually collecting information from different systems to investigate support issues. Reporting also required data to be gathered manually from multiple sources.

 2. Where and why did they implement n8n?

Oversee implemented n8n as a **workflow orchestration layer** to connect different systems and automate repetitive support investigations and reporting. They wanted to improve productivity without increasing headcount.

 3. How was the workflow implemented?

When a support request comes in, n8n collects relevant information from different systems, uses AI to analyze the information, and creates a structured report. n8n is also used to collect data and generate reports from multiple sources based on requests from employees.

 4. How did n8n help in real-world scenarios?

* Reduced support ticket first-response time by **50%**.
* Reports that previously took around **2 weeks** could be produced in **2–3 hours**.
* Around **20 employees** use n8n-enabled workflows.
* The 8-person operations team uses n8n-powered tools.

Key takeaway: n8n helped Oversee reduce manual investigation and reporting work, allowing employees to focus more on solving customer issues.

Case Study 2: System AI
-----------------------
 1. What problem/use case did they have?

System AI's real estate client had repetitive administrative work. Employees and virtual assistants manually copied information from **WhatsApp into Zoho CRM and Google Sheets**, which took several minutes for each task.

 2. Where and why did they implement n8n?

System AI implemented n8n to automate data entry and connect **WhatsApp, Twilio, AI services, Zoho CRM, and Google Sheets**. They chose n8n to reduce manual work and build scalable AI-powered workflows.

 3. How was the workflow implemented?

The workflow starts with a WhatsApp message. n8n identifies whether it is text or a voice message, uses AI to understand the request, and then routes it to the appropriate workflow:

Lead request → Zoho CRM
Property request → Google Sheets
A confirmation is then sent back through WhatsApp.

 4. How did n8n help in real-world scenarios?

* Reduced task time from **4–5 minutes to 10–20 seconds**.
* Achieved a **97% reduction in operation time**.
* Saved approximately **one day of work every week**.
* Reduced the time from property onboarding to sale from **62 days to 44 days**.
* Virtual assistants could focus on higher-value work instead of repetitive data entry.

Key takeaway: n8n automated repetitive data-entry tasks and connected AI with existing business applications, making the real estate process much faster and more efficient.signement
