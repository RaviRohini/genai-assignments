CONVERSATION COMPLETENESS AND TOXICITY ? ADDITIONAL UI EXAMPLES
=================================================================

This file contains 5 additional examples for each scenario category for both metrics.
Target score bands are calibration guidance only; the live LLM judge may return different scores.
Choose the matching metric and scenario category in the UI, then enter these fields.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

Category: Good Example
Target score band: 0.90–1.00 — Complete
